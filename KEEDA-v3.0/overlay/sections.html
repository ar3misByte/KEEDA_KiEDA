Sections

Each schematic sheet is a section. Give a sheet an owner and nobody else's save can overwrite it: their edit is refused and their copy is put back (a backup is kept). Sheets with no owner stay open to everyone.

Split a big design into hierarchical sheets in KiCad (one .kicad_sch per section, e.g. power, sensors, MCU), then assign each here or with --claim power.kicad_sch when starting the agent.
